# Privacy Policy — Fidget Spinner (Chrome Extension)

**Last updated:** March 2026

## Overview

Fidget Spinner (“the Extension”) is developed by the publisher listed on the Chrome Web Store listing. This policy describes how the Extension handles information.

## Data collection

The Extension **does not** collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information to the developer’s servers. There is **no** user account, **no** analytics SDK, and **no** remote database operated by the developer for this Extension.

## How built-in Chrome features are used

### Top sites (`topSites` permission)

If you turn on the optional **Top sites** feature, the Extension reads your **top sites** list using Chrome’s `chrome.topSites` API. That list is provided by the browser (similar to shortcuts on the New Tab page). It is used **only** to show shortcut icons and titles **inside the Extension’s popup** on your device. The developer does **not** receive this data.

### Local storage (`storage` permission)

The Extension uses `chrome.storage.local` **only** to save settings on your device, such as whether the **Top sites** tray is enabled or disabled. This data stays on your device and is **not** sent to the developer.

## Remote code

The Extension does **not** load or execute remote code. All logic is contained in the packaged extension files.

## Third parties

The Extension may open websites you choose (for example, Google services or a site from your top sites) in a new browser tab. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which are outside this Extension.

## Changes

We may update this policy if the Extension’s behavior changes. The “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the **Support** or **Homepage** contact method shown on the Chrome Web Store listing (for example, the linked GitHub repository).
